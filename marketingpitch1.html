The Opportunity

Most sober living homes in Dallas are overcrowded, poorly maintained, and placed with no strategic thought about resident outcomes. Lily Pad Living is built on the opposite premise: premium-condition homes, intentionally located, professionally managed.

Recovery housing is one of the most undersupplied resources in Dallas — providing structured, supportive housing for individuals rebuilding their lives, while generating strong, predictable returns for investors.

The long-term vision is a concentrated cluster of 20–30 Lily Pad properties within the same zip code — allowing residents to form genuine community, strengthening accountability and recovery outcomes, while making operations significantly more efficient.

Why 75203

Parkland Health — One of the largest safety-net hospitals in the US. Trauma care, behavioral health, and detox referrals feed directly into recovery housing demand.

Metrocare Services — Dallas's primary community mental health authority. Outpatient therapy, psychiatric care, and case management — all within easy reach.

DART Transit Access — With a transit pass and a scooter or bike, virtually all of Dallas is accessible: jobs, groceries, support groups, courts. No car required.

Community by Design — Concentrating properties in one zip code means residents build real relationships and support each other's recovery — turning a portfolio into a recovery ecosystem.
