Dallas area housing

Affordable, structured housing in the DFW area.

Our houses are designed to be clean, safe, and well-managed — with enough structure to make them actually work for people who are trying to get stable.

Pricing

We keep pricing simple and weekly so that it's accessible without requiring a large upfront commitment. All utilities are included.

Weekly Rent

Exact rate depends on the house and room. All utilities, WiFi, and basic household supplies included.

Move-In

First week's rent to move in

No large security deposit. We want the barrier to entry to be low for people who are doing the right things.

Payment

Due every Monday

Weekly payments keep things predictable for residents and for us. Accepted via cash, Zelle, or Venmo.

House Rules & Expectations

These rules apply to all Lily Pad Living houses. They're not meant to be difficult — they're meant to protect everyone in the house, including you.

Sobriety On Premises

No alcohol or illegal drugs on the property. This is non-negotiable and applies to residents and guests alike.

Employment or Active Search

All residents must be working, actively job searching, or enrolled in a program. Productive participation is required.

Respect Common Areas

Clean up after yourself in the kitchen and shared spaces. A chore rotation will be posted in each house.

Guest Policy

Guests must be pre-approved and are not permitted to stay overnight without explicit permission from management.

Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are 10pm–7am on weekdays, 11pm–8am on weekends. Respect your housemates' rest.

Weekly Check-Ins

All residents participate in a brief weekly house meeting. Issues get resolved early, not ignored.
