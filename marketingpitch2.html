Why This Works
Demand is structural Recovery housing in Dallas is critically undersupplied. Parkland, Metrocare, 24 Hour Club, and Homeward Bound generate consistent referral demand that isn't going away.
Location is intentional 75203 puts residents within reach of healthcare, mental health services, transit, and employment — removing barriers to recovery and reducing turnover.
Premium positioning Most Dallas sober living is in poor condition and scattered throughout the city. Clean, well-managed homes at accessible rates are extremely attractive to residents and will keep referrals coming so occupancy remains high.
Community by design Concentrating homes in one zip code turns a portfolio into a recovery ecosystem — residents support each other, reducing management burden and improving outcomes.
Low ongoing costs Xeriscape yards eliminate irrigation and reduce landscaping to a single annual cleanup. No lawn. No mowing. Designed to stay lean.
Conservative underwriting Cash flow positive at 7 residents. Full occupancy at 8 produces a strong monthly surplus, with equity building through principal paydown regardless of occupancy.

Addendum: 2nd Story Addition (Alternative Where Lot Size Prevents Ground Expansion)

When a property's lot size or setback requirements prevent a ground-floor addition, a full 2nd story is the recommended alternative. Adds square footage without reducing yard space and typically increases resale value, but carries higher upfront costs and a longer construction timeline (6–12 months vs. 3–5 months).
